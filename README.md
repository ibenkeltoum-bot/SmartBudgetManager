# SmartBudgetManager
A project for managing Budget
What is SmartBudget Manager?
SmartBudget Manager is an Excel workbook that helps you record monthly income and expenses, group spending into categories, and monitor your budget from a dashboard.
What you need
Microsoft Excel 2016, 2019, 2021, Microsoft 365, or a compatible spreadsheet application.
Workbook sheets
Dashboard: view total income, total expenses, monthly balance, budget status, category summary, and chart.
Transactions: enter each income or expense as a separate row.
Categories: review the predefined categories and their monthly budget amounts.
Getting started
1.Open SmartBudget_Manager.xlsx.
2.Go to Transactions and enter the date, description, type, category, and amount in the appropriate column.
3.Use Income for money received and Expense for money spent. Enter the amount in the matching Income or Expense column.
4.Review or adjust category budgets in Categories, if needed.
5.Return to Dashboard to review the updated totals and budget status.
6.Save your own copy using a new filename.
Important
Do not overwrite formula cells. Keep entries within the existing transaction table and check the Dashboard after entering data. The workbook is a manual tracker and does not connect to a bank account.
